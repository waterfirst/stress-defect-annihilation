# Figure Captions (COPP 260089)

**Fig. 1.** Full specimen matrix with representative top-view and cross-sectional micrographs of the picosecond Bessel-beam-drilled glass (one mapped specimen per indexed drilling condition).

**Fig. 2.** Representative 550-nm retardance maps for Low, Middle, and High pulse-energy categories at 20-µm pitch and nominal through-hole depth; retardance strengthens and becomes more heterogeneous with increasing pulse energy.

**Fig. 3.** High-energy retardance maps and line profiles for the 20- and 10-µm pitch series across target depths of 300, 400, and 500 µm; the 400-µm condition yields the largest representative peak.

**Fig. 4.** Six retained slow-axis (director-field) maps across process conditions, showing organized bands and local rotations around the hole row rather than random orientation.

**Fig. 5.** Slow-axis map, microscope image of an inter-hole crack, and a higher-resolution orientation map from the same specimen; the crack tangent is approximately transverse to nearby slow-axis segments over part of its path (qualitative correspondence only).

**Fig. 6.** Reduced-order field evolution for same- and opposite-polarity controls at equal colour scale (h/p = 0.55, Da = 0.5).

**Fig. 7.** Design map of the 90% quadratic-field-content reduction threshold versus normalized mobility radius, isolating the threshold behaviour from the long-exposure tail.

**Fig. 8.** Grid-refinement verification, Damköhler-number sensitivity, and the conditional conversion from Fourier number (Fo) to physical time; increasing Da shifts the reference Fo90 for both polarity controls.
